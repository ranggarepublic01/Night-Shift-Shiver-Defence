# Night Defenders: Monster Tower Defense — store page

**Title:** Night Defenders: Monster Tower Defense

**Short description (one line):**
A cozy-spooky monster tower defense: the monster clinic's night shift keeps the Dawn away from the curtains for one more hour.

**Description:**
It's the last hour before sunrise, and the monster clinic wants one more hour of night. The Dawn is sending its little
helpers along the garden path to open the clinic's curtains: humming Sunbeams, sprinting Roosters, Alarm Clocks that ring
louder the more you hit them, cheerful Morning Joggers, Early Birds, Paper Pigeons with the morning paper, and finally the
sleepy Sun itself.

Put the clinic's staff and patients on the stones beside the path and shoo them all back to bed. Nobody gets hurt: every
Dawn helper just puffs away into sparkles.

- Five monsters, each with its own job: Rattle throws bones, Wisp's cold breath slows everyone, Dr. Frankenstein's zap
  jumps between helpers, Gourdon lobs bursting pumpkins, and Vesper's bats catch the birds.
- Three upgrade levels per monster, and every upgrade changes how it looks: crowns, capes, coils and circling bats.
- Three maps: the Garden Path, the Pumpkin Patch and the Rooftop. Each one adds a new monster.
- Earn up to 3 stars per map by keeping every candle lit.
- Endless mode: waves forever. How many can you hold?
- Leaderboards for total stars and the best Endless wave.

**How to play:**
Tap a stone beside the path and pick a monster, then tap it again to place it. Tap a placed monster to upgrade or sell
it. Start each wave with the sunrise button by the gate; calling a wave early earns bonus candy. Every helper that
reaches the window blows out a candle. Lose all 20 and the clinic wakes up.

**Controls:** tap or click. Landscape only.
